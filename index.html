About Me

Hi! My name is Gracelyn Deputy, and I am currently a senior at Iowa State University studying marketing and management. Before coming to Iowa State, I spent my first two years at Iowa Lakes Community College, where I earned my AA degree.

Throughout college, I have had a lot of different experiences that have helped me learn more about myself and what I enjoy. One of the biggest parts of my college experience was being a member of the women's basketball team at Iowa Lakes. Being part of a team taught me a lot about communication, responsibility, teamwork, and pushing myself even when things get difficult.

Outside of school, I enjoy staying busy and working with people. I currently work two jobs. I work as a server at a restaurant, and I also work for Friendship Ark, where I support adults with intellectual disabilities. Working at Friendship Ark has especially helped me realize how much I enjoy being around people and doing work that feels meaningful.

My experiences have also helped me learn more about what I want in the future. I enjoy marketing and the creative side of business, but I also really value being able to make an impact on other people. I am still figuring out exactly what I want to do after graduation, but I am excited to keep learning, gaining experience, and seeing where my career takes me.

When I am not working or doing schoolwork, I enjoy spending time with my roommates, friends, and the people around me. I like staying busy, trying new things, and making the most out of my college experience.
