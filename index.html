About Me

Hi! My name is Gracelyn Deputy, and I am currently a senior at Iowa State University studying marketing and management. Before coming to Iowa State, I spent my first two years at Iowa Lakes Community College, where I earned my AA degree.

My college experience has given me a lot of opportunities to learn more about myself, both inside and outside of the classroom. While attending Iowa Lakes, I was a member of the women's basketball team. Being part of a team taught me a lot about communication, teamwork, responsibility, and working toward a common goal.

Outside of school, I stay pretty busy with work. I currently have two jobs. I work as a server at a restaurant, and I also work for Friendship Ark, where I support adults with intellectual disabilities.

Working at Friendship Ark has especially helped me realize how much I enjoy being around people and doing work that feels meaningful. I originally thought of it as just another job, but the experience has taught me a lot about the kind of work I actually enjoy.

I enjoy marketing because I like the creative side of business and getting to understand people. At the same time, I have realized that making an impact on others is something that is really important to me. I am still figuring out exactly what I want to do after graduation, but I am excited to continue gaining experience and learning more about what career is right for me.

When I am not working or doing schoolwork, I enjoy spending time with my roommates, friends, and the people around me. I like staying busy, trying new things, and making the most out of my college experience.
