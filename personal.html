A Little More About Me

When I’m not at school or work, I’m usually spending time with my friends and roommates, staying active, or finding something fun to do. I love having people around me and making memories, whether that means going out, trying something new, or just hanging out at home.

Basketball has also been a big part of my life. From playing throughout high school and college to being part of a team, it has given me some of my favorite memories and taught me a lot about friendship, teamwork, and pushing myself.

Some of My Favorite Things

  • Spending time with friends and family
  • Basketball and staying active
  • Making memories with my roommates
  • Going out and trying new things
  • Listening to music
  • Traveling and exploring new places
  • Enjoying the little things in everyday life
